Natural colour, never bleached

How to spot pure jaggery

Bright yellow jaggery is often bleached with hydros. Ours keeps the natural golden-brown colour of cane juice.

  • ColourGolden-brown to dark brown is natural. Bright yellow is a warning sign.
  • Water testDissolve a piece in water. Chalk or fillers settle at the bottom.
  • TextureSlightly grainy and uneven, never glossy.

From sugarcane to jaggery

Six steps, done by hand at our own unit in Surat. No chemicals or preservatives at any stage.

~10 kgjaggery from every 100 kg of cane
2–3 hrsof slow boiling for each batch
0chemicals, bleach or preservatives
CaneJuiceSyrupJaggery
  1. 1

    Harvest

    Fresh, mature sugarcane

    Ripe cane is cut on the farm. Tops and dry leaves are trimmed off so only clean, juicy stalks go to the crusher.

    10–12 month cropCut by hand

  2. 2

    Crushing

    Pressing out the juice

    The stalks pass through heavy rollers that squeeze out fresh, sweet juice. Cane is crushed soon after cutting, before the juice starts to lose its sweetness.

    Within 24 hours of cutting

  3. 3

    Filtering

    Straining the juice

    The juice is strained through fine mesh and cloth to take out fibre, soil and small particles before it reaches the pan.

    Clean juice only

  4. 4

    Slow boiling

    Boiling and cleaning

    The juice boils in large open iron pans. Impurities rise as foam and are skimmed off by hand, again and again. No hydros, soda or bleach is used to clean or lighten it.

    2–3 hoursOpen panNo chemical cleaning

  5. 5

    Thickening

    The striking point

    Stirred non-stop until the syrup turns thick and golden. Our cooks check it by hand: a drop in water should set firm. Spices or dry fruits are folded in here for our flavoured range.

    About 118°CChecked by hand

  6. 6

    Setting and packing

    Shaped, cooled, sealed

    The hot syrup is spread to cool, then pressed into 1 kg blocks, shaped into small pieces or ground into powder. Once fully set and dry, it is packed in sealed Rahul Farm 1 kg and 4 kg buckets and jars.

    BlocksPiecesPowder1 kg & 4 kg buckets
