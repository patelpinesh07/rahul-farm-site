About this jaggery

Our desi gud ground into a fine powder and packed in a clear jar with a screw lid. It measures like sugar and mixes in easily, with no grating or breaking.

Nothing is added while making it: no hydros, soda, bleach or preservatives. A simple way to use jaggery in place of sugar every day.

How to use

  • Sweeten tea, coffee and milk
  • Use in place of sugar in sweets and desserts
  • Bake cakes, cookies and laddoos
  • Sprinkle over porridge, curd or fruit

How to spot pure jaggery

Colour

Golden-brown to dark brown is natural. Bright yellow is a warning sign.

Water test

Dissolve a piece in water. Chalk or fillers settle at the bottom.

Texture

Slightly grainy and uneven, never glossy.

Ask a question
Can I use it in place of sugar?

Yes, in tea, sweets and most baking. Start with the same amount and adjust to taste.

Why does it sometimes form lumps?

Jaggery powder takes in moisture from the air. Keep the lid tight and use a dry spoon.
