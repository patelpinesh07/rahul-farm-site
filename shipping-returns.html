Shipping and returns

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Where we deliver

We deliver across India. Home orders go by courier. Bulk orders go by courier or transport, depending on the quantity and your city.

Dispatch and delivery time

Orders are dispatched within after your order and payment are confirmed.

We share the dispatch date on WhatsApp when we confirm your order.

Most orders reach you within of dispatch. Remote PIN codes can take longer.

We send you the courier name and tracking details on WhatsApp once your order is on its way.

Shipping charges

Shipping depends on the weight of your order and your PIN code. We tell you the exact charge on WhatsApp before you pay, so there are no surprises.

Damaged, leaking or wrong items

Jaggery is a food product, so we can't take back opened packs. If your order arrives damaged, leaking or with the wrong item, send us photos of the parcel and the product on WhatsApp within 48 hours of delivery. We will replace the item or refund you.

Refunds

Approved refunds are sent to the same payment method you used, usually within 7 working days.

Cancellations

You can cancel any time before your order is dispatched. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll refund the full amount.
