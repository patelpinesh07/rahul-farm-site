Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains what information Rahul Farm ("we", "us") collects when you visit this website or contact us, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Details you send us on WhatsApp, by phone or by email, such as your name, shop name, city, phone number and delivery address.
  • The products and quantities you ask about or order.
  • Basic visit data collected by analytics and advertising tools, such as pages viewed, device type and the ad you came from.

How we use it

  • To send you prices and quotes, take your order and deliver it.
  • To reply to your questions and give support after delivery.
  • To understand which ads and pages work, so we can improve them.

Cookies, Meta Pixel and analytics

This website may use the Meta Pixel and Google Analytics. These tools use cookies to measure visits and the results of our Facebook and Instagram ads. You can block cookies in your browser settings and manage ad preferences in your Facebook or Instagram account.

Who we share it with

We share your name, phone number and address with our courier or transport partner only to deliver your order. We do not sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep order and contact details for as long as we need them to serve you and to meet tax and legal requirements.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Contact us using the details below.

Changes
